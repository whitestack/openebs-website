## Slack

You can reach out to OpenEBS contributors and maintainers through following [Kubernetes Slack](https://slack.k8s.io) Slack Channels. If you are already signed up to Kubernetes slack, join the following channels:

- Join the #openebs for user discussions.
- Join the #openebs-dev for contributor discussions.

## Blogs

Community blogs are available at https://openebs.io/blog/.

## Community Meetings

Join our weekly or monthly community meetings.
